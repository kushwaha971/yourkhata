Bug fixes, your feedback, and what changes for each kind of shop

This page keeps the Shop exactly as it looks today. It only draws the parts that change: the problems in your screenshots, the four points parked in the backlog, and the new parts from the business-type plan (picking what you sell, scanning, sizes, batch and expiry, IMEI and the rest). Each fix shows your screenshot next to the fixed phone screen and the desktop screen.

Use Business type at the top to switch between eight kinds of shop. The menus, the words, the bill lines, the item form and the items list redraw for that shop. Dashed orange outlines mark a word or menu row that changed for that business. Everything else stays generic.

What this version adds

Nothing above was redrawn without a reason. This version adds research, two shop kinds and the screens each kind was missing, in the same components.

  • Part R, research: for each of the 10 shop kinds, who the competitors are, what a shop of that kind must have, how competitors do it, where they fall short, and where YourKhata does better. Every must-have is marked In the app, Designed, New here or Later.

Part R

Competitor research per shop kind

Indian and global products each kind of shop uses today, read from their own pages, buyer guides and reviews (sources at the end of this part; vendor-written guides are marked as such in our notes and were not taken at their word). "In the app" means it works on dev today.

R2 · Patterns across every kind

What every competitor trains shops to expect

  • GST bill with a thermal or A4 print and a WhatsApp share
  • Barcode scan at the counter
  • Udhaar with reminders, and UPI on the bill
  • A free or cheap mobile app, with the desktop sold apart

Where they all fall short

  • Every kind sees every feature, so the screen is crowded (Vyapar, myBillBook)
  • Specialist tools (Marg, GoFrugal, jewellery ERPs) need dealers, AMC and weeks of setup, and are weak on the phone
  • Ledger apps and billing apps are separate, so shops run two
  • Quote-only or rising renewal prices

How YourKhata wins, kind by kind

  • One kind per business: only that kind's menu, words and fields (R-DEC-65)
  • Specialist depth inside the simple counter bill: batch picked by itself, IMEI scanned, weight priced
  • Bill and udhaar in one app, phone first, desktop the same
  • No paid feeds or providers (ADR-021): rates typed, messages shared, not sent

One rule for every folding section

  1. No inner box. What is inside a folding section is drawn bare. Groups inside are split by a 1 px line, with the same 16 px side padding as the header. On the customer page, Terms, Notes and Record become three rows of the one box.
  2. The header has no grey fill when open or closed. Grey is only the hover and press colour.
  3. Corners: the outer box clips its children (overflow: clip). The header button no longer has its own round corners, so a hover or focus colour fills right to the edge and into the corners.
  4. Focus ring goes on the whole box, not on the header, so it follows the outer corners (the Edit contact screenshot shows the ring cut by the body).
  5. Closed shows the summary under the title ("25/10/2026 · Maharashtra (27)"). Open keeps it, so you can still read it while editing.

Owner, 30 Oct: picking another business should feel like opening a new app. The business switcher at the top left lists each business with its kind. Picking one reloads everything for it: name and initial, menu, words, dashboard, fields, settings and data. The colours, fonts and components stay the same, so it is clearly the same product.

Phone · tap the business name
Desktop · the switcher in the sidebar

What keeps each business separate (integrity)

  • Data never crosses. Customers, suppliers, items, stock, bills, payments, udhaar, GSTIN, bank and UPI, bill numbers, staff and every setting belong to one business. Search, reports and exports only ever see the business that is open.
  • One kind per business (R-DEC-64). The kind's preset sets the feature switches; screens check a switch, never the kind (R-DEC-65). Wholesale is a switch on a kind.
  • Switching is a clean start. Open drafts stay with their business. The page reloads into the new business's Home, with a note "You're now in Sai Medical Store". The address and the tab title carry the business, so two tabs can never mix.
  • Staff see only the businesses they were added to, with that business's role.
  • The server checks too. Every request carries the business and every row is filtered by it. A link from another business opens "Not in this business", never its data.

What stays the same in every business (consistency)

  • Same design system: teal and marigold, Plus Jakarta Sans, the same buttons, sheets, drawers, tables and count line.
  • The menu keeps the same order everywhere. A kind only adds or leaves out rows (decisions 1 and 6), so moving between businesses never means hunting for Bills.
  • The same bottom bar on phone: Home, Customers, Suppliers, Bills, More. Only the words change (decision 2).
