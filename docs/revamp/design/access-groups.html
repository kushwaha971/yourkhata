What this page is

Today the owner sets each member's apps and parts one by one. An access group is a named set of those choices, made once. Put five counter staff in "Counter staff" and change the group once: all five change.

RuleWhat it means for the shopkeeper
Each business has its own groups"Counter staff" in one shop and "Counter staff" in another are separate. Each owner makes their own. A person who works in two businesses can be in a different group in each.
Three levels per partOff (not shown), View only (can see, cannot make or change) and Full (can make and change). Parts that are only an action, like Take fees or Give loans, have Off and Full.
One default groupPicked already when you add a member. You can change it there.
Exceptions stay possibleA member follows their group, and the owner can still change one part for one person. The screen says so, with "Use group again".
Never in a groupDeleting or cancelling a bill stays with the owner and admins. Money book is each person's own and is not in groups.
Who can change groupsThe owner and admins.

Questions for you

Each has my suggestion. Say yes, or tell me what to change.

QuestionSuggestion
Name of the tab"Access groups". Hindi: "पहुँच समूह".
Groups and rolesKeep Admin, Staff and Accountant as they are. A group only narrows what a Staff or Accountant member can do; it never gives more than their role. Admins are not put in groups.
Ready-made groupOffer one example on the empty tab, "Counter staff" (Sales full, Payments full, Stock view only, Purchases and Expenses off), which the owner can change before saving.
Deleting a groupAllowed only when nobody is in it; otherwise it asks to move its members to another group first.
ReportsNot a part for now: the Dashboard uses the same permission, so hiding Reports would hide the member's home screen.
