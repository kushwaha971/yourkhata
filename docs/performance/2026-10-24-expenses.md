Scope: `/expenses` (`ExpensesPageContent`, `ExpenseColumns`, `ExpenseListStats`,
`useExpenseList`, `expenseListSlice`), the Add expense drawer
(`ExpenseFormDrawer`, `ExpenseCategoryField`, `useExpenseForm`) and the expense
detail with its cancel dialog (`ExpenseDetailDrawer`, `VoidExpenseDialog`). The
Cash book (§11) is NOT covered; it is the next piece.

The Library opens the same `/expenses` page for its own expenses
(`expenseAppOf`, `app=library`). Its requests and data are unchanged; the page
layout and the words are shared, so it gets the new register too (recorded
below).

Who needs it: the shopkeeper noting money that goes out (tea, rent, porter)
so the cash book tallies, and the owner at month end asking where money went.

## Server check (DEC-019)

The spec asked to verify that an unpaid expense posts in the Shop book. It
does: the expense ledger source registers only the Shop bucket
(`backend/apps/expenses/apps.py:33`), and `post_source_entry` refuses any
other (`apps/ledger/services/postings.py`). The live run proves it again: a
₹1 expense owed to Bose Kirana 1460 moved their `shop_balance` from
−17,396.00 to −17,397.00, and cancelling it put it back. Only the words were
wrong ("in the khata of {name}").

## What changed

- **The register (R-DEC-33).** Joined cards that fill the screen under the
  controls with the next page appended as the list is scrolled, and a table
  that pages inside its frame on a desktop, the same as Payments. The slice
  keeps the cards while the next page loads and lets only the latest
  request's answer in.
- **A card says what the money went on first.** The category is the title,
  then "EXP/26-27/0548 · UPI · 17/10/2026", then the note, then an Unpaid
  badge with "owed to …" or a Cancelled badge. Before, the note was the title
  and the category was not on a phone card at all.
- **Total and count on one line**, "Total ₹1,21,917.00 · 47 expenses", and
  the top three categories under it (the server sends five).
- **Chips All · Unpaid · Cancelled**, visible on a phone as on a desktop.
- **Words.** "Void" is "Cancelled" everywhere in Expenses; "Cashbook" is
  "Cash book" (§0.3); an unpaid expense says "This is added to what the Shop
  owes {name}." and "The Shop owes {name} this money." instead of "the
  khata of"; "Pay to" / "Due on" are "Owed to" / "Pay by"; "Save & add
  another" is "Save and add another"; the empty book says "No expenses yet";
  a quiet period says "No expenses in this period".
- **Add expense.** The six most-used categories are one-tap chips (the
  server already sends them most-used first); the rest are in "More
  categories" when there are more than six. Nothing is red until Save. The
  saved message names the number.
- **Cancel expense** says what changes before it happens, in Shop words:
  "It leaves the cash book for 17/10/2026" for a paid one, "₹1.00 comes off
  what the Shop owes Bose Kirana 1460" for an unpaid one, with quick reasons
  and Keep. The detail then says who cancelled it and when.

## Found by looking and by the live run

1. **"Save and add another" never put the caret back in Amount.**
   `form.setFocus('amount')` needs a ref and `UbField` gives its input none,
   so the call did nothing, and nothing had ever tested it. The drawer now
   focuses the input by its id, and the drawer test checks the focus.
2. **"New category" covered the lower part of two category chips' targets**
   at 390 px. A 28 px button's 44 px target reaches 8 px above it, and a chip's
   reaches 6 px below, so a 12 px gap still overlapped. The gap is 16 px.
3. **The top categories took two lines at 390 px and three at 320 px**, all
   of it room the cards needed. On a phone it is one line that scrolls
   sideways; the harness measures its height.
4. **At 1024 px the filters wrapped to a second row**, and once they fitted,
   the search hint was cut to "Number, note or c". Below 1280 px the two
   selects show only their value ("All categories", "All modes"); the
   accessible name keeps "Category:" and "Paid by:". The harness now measures
   the placeholder against its box, which the text-node check cannot see.

## Deviations and decisions recorded

1. **Add expense is in the title row on a phone**, not a floating button:
   the later register decision (R-DEC-33) puts the main action there, as on
   Payments.
2. **Chips, not tabs**, visible on a phone, as on Payments.
3. **Dates on cards are dd/mm/yyyy**, not "17 Oct" (the product's date rule).
4. **"What the Shop owes {name}"**, not "{name}'s Shop balance": the
   template cannot know whether a name takes "'" or "'s" ("Gupta Oil
   Mills's"), and the receipt's cancel dialog already says "The Shop owes
   {name}".
5. **The Library opens this same page** for its own expenses (`app=library`,
   unchanged). It gets the new register and words too; its cancel dialog
   names no cash book ("It comes off your expenses for {date}"), because the
   Library has none. Its requests and money are unchanged.
6. **Five ₹1 expenses are left cancelled on the QA book per run** (notes
   "QA89"), because an expense is never deleted. The cash book is unchanged.

## Performance

Same probe as Payments (`data/2026-10-24-expenses/exab.sh`, `experf.mjs`):
the production build of dcaddbc (old) against this build (new), alternated
over four rounds, six opens per viewport per round, 4x CPU throttle, timed
from navigation to the first visible card or table row. n = 24 per cell.

| | old median (range) | new median (range) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone 390 | 1,336 ms (1,127–1,921) | 1,357 ms (1,066–2,172) | level (+1.6%) |
| Desktop 1280 | 975 ms (866–1,319) | 1,060 ms (883–2,334) | slower (+8.7%) |

The desktop is slower, as it was on Payments (918 → 1,028). The likely
cause is the shared register shell measuring its frame before the grid
paints, plus more in each row; that is inferred from the Payments result,
not traced for this page. The ranges overlap and one 2,334 ms
outlier sits in the new set, but the median moved and is recorded as slower,
not explained away. Raw numbers are in `data/2026-10-24-expenses/ex-*.json`.
