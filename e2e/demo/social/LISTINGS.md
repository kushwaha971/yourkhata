## 2. Quora (answer real questions; a link only where it is the answer)

Search Quora for these and answer the newest unanswered ones. Write as yourself, the founder,
and say so ("I make YourKhata") whenever the product is mentioned.

**Q: "Which is the best app for udhaar / khata for a small shop?"**
> Whichever one you will actually open every time someone takes credit. Three things matter more
> than features: it should open fast on a cheap phone, writing an entry should take two taps,
> and it should help you ask for the money (a WhatsApp message with the exact amount and dates
> works better than asking face to face). Try two or three apps for a week each with your real
> customers before deciding. (I make YourKhata, which has a free personal Money Book for exactly
> this — yourkhata.com/hi/solutions/money-book-udhaar — but test it against the others.)

**Q: "How do I make a GST bill for a customer in another state?"**
> Use IGST at the full rate instead of splitting it into CGST + SGST. Same state = CGST + SGST
> (half each). Different state = IGST (whole rate). Show the place of supply and the buyer's
> GSTIN if they are registered. Not tax advice; for exports or SEZ ask your CA.
> (No link; this one is just a helpful answer.)

**Q: "How do small finance / daily collection businesses keep records?"**
> Most still use a line register. It breaks when someone pays half, misses days, or an amount
> is miswritten. Whatever you use, record every visit (even when nothing is paid, with the
> reason and promised day), apply money to the oldest due first, and close the day by counting
> what is still out per route. (I make YourKhata's Ledger app for this; the guide with real
> screens is at yourkhata.com/solutions/lending-collections.)

**Q: "Free software for a self-study library: seats, fees, attendance?"**
> Keep one record per member with their seat, plan and fee due date; mark attendance daily; and
> send fee reminders before the due date, not after. (YourKhata's Library app does this free
> to start — yourkhata.com/solutions/library-management.)
