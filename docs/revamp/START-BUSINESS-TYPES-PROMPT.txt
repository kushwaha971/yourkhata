────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE BUSINESS-TYPE MODEL THE OWNER CHOSE (do not redesign it)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- The business type chosen at sign-up decides what shows: features, fields,
  starter categories and units, and wording. Sign-up asks "What do you sell?"
  with picture tiles (EN + HI): Kirana, Medical, Mobile, Kapde, Hardware,
  Mithai/Bakery, Wholesale, Jewellery, Other.
- Users won't go looking in Settings. Features arrive from the preset at
  sign-up, from the owner's team in the platform console, or as a one-time yes/no
  card at the moment of need (UbFeatureOffer). Settings › Shop features is only a fallback.
- Sidebar, menu and screen wording follow the business type where it helps
  ("Retailer / Company" for a distributor, "Client / Vendor" for services,
  "Distributor" as a medical store's supplier). Keep generic words everywhere
  else. Wording comes from preset-written label settings (like `party_labels`
  today), never from `if business_type == …` in code.
- Every new feature is OFF for existing businesses. A kirana sees exactly what it
  sees today.
- Shop flavours (kirana, medical, mobile, garments, hardware) are ONE Shop with
  extra fields. Jewellery (later Clinic, Accounts, Production) are separate apps in
  More/Apps. Separate businesses use the existing Business switcher. A mixed shop
  under one GSTIN can choose more than one type, and fields can be scoped by item category.
