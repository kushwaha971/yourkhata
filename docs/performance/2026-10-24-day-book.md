# Day book · 24 Oct 2026 (09 §10.2, R-DEC-32, R-DEC-34)

Scope: `/reports/day-book` (`DayBookPageContent`, `DayBookDayGroups`,
`DayBookColumns`, the new `DayBookRowSheet`) and two pieces it shares with the
cash book: the ruled figure box (`src/components/stats/FigureBox.tsx`, was
`CashbookFigures`) and the helpers for a figure inside a sentence and a weekday
(`src/utils/moneyUnbroken.ts`, `src/utils/weekday.ts`). The server is unchanged.

Who needs it: the owner at closing time tallying the drawer ("everything that
happened today, in order, with the cash after each line"), and the accountant
posting a month. What it saves them: the four figures without a tap, a day's
in and out on its band, and each line readable at a glance on a phone, with the
running cash one tap away instead of on every card.

## What changed

- **The answer first.** Money in, Money out, Cash at close and Bank at close
  in the cash book's ruled box (2 × 2 on a phone, one line on a desktop), each
  closing with "opened at …" under it, a negative one in error tone. It
  replaces five stat cards folded behind "Details".
- **A band per date** with the weekday, and that day's In and Out on the right.
  When a long range is paged, a band reads "On this page: In … · Out …" rather
  than presenting part of a day as all of it.
- **Cards of three things**: the contact (or Quick Sale, or the type for a
  line with nobody), the time with "Bill INV/26-27/0418" / "Expense: Tea", and
  one amount with its word: In (green), Out, or for a bill that moved no cash,
  Paid / On credit / Part paid. A cancelled line is struck through and says so.
- **The row sheet**, from a card on a phone and from the first cell of a table
  row on a desktop: type, date and time, number, contact, description, in, out,
  mode, cash and bank after (only for a reader who may see them), who recorded
  it, and Open for the document and the contact.
- **Words**: Show cancelled (was Show voided), Cancelled (was Void), Payment
  received / Payment made, Bill, Quick Sale, Cash at close, Cash after.

## Found by looking and by the live run

1. **The desktop table cut almost every figure**, at 1280 as well as 1024:
   "01/1…", "15:…", "-12,7…" in the cash column, names cut to one word. Twelve
   columns in a fixed-layout table; a running cash figure needs about 118 px
   and had 56–75. Nothing in the unit tests could see it. The table is now six
   columns sized for 1024 (When · Type and number · Contact · Amount · Cash
   after · Bank after); Description, Mode and By moved to the row sheet.
2. **At 320 px Hindi a bill number broke in two** ("बिल INV/26-" / "27/10028"),
   and **the opening cash's minus left its number** ("शुरू में -" /
   "₹12,76,735.04"). Each piece of the line now stays whole, captions use the
   non-breaking minus, and the harness checks both with text ranges.
3. **Two helpers grew every sales editor by 0.4 KB.** `weekdayShort` and the
   unbroken money format were first put in `dates.ts` and `money.ts`, which
   every editor imports; the editors went over budget. They moved to modules
   of their own and the editors are back under their unchanged budgets.

## Deviations and decisions recorded

1. **Four figures, not three.** The spec's phone line is Money in · Money out ·
   Cash at close; Bank at close is the fourth because the drawer question has
   two halves and the 2 × 2 box would otherwise have a hole. Opening is the
   caption under each closing figure.
2. **"Open as full table" is gone on a phone.** It scrolled the wide table
   sideways inside the page, which §10.0 rules out; every column is in the row
   sheet and Download gives the whole table.
3. **The desktop table is six columns, not eight.** In and Out are one Amount
   column said in words (as on the phone card), date and time one cell, type
   and number one cell: eight separate columns still cut at 1024 (see finding 1).
4. **The no-permission hint keeps its accurate wording**: "visible to owners,
   admins and accountants", not the spec's "to the owner", because the server
   grants it with `reports.financial.read`, which those roles hold.
5. **No Print.** No report has a Print button, and printing this page would
   print whichever layout the window happened to be in. A print sheet for
   reports belongs in `ReportPageShell` once, for all of them; Download covers
   the full table meanwhile.
6. **"You gave" / "You got"** remain the labels of a hand-written khata line in
   the Shop's book. The QA shop has none; renaming them is not invented here.
7. **The harness writes to the local QA book**: each run posts one ₹1 cash
   entry in the Udhaar book and reverses it, which leaves both rows visible
   there with "Show corrections".
8. **Bundle**: `/reports/day-book` first load 360.0 → 360.4 KB and
   `/reports/gst-summary` route 116.5 → 116.9 KB. The GST route imports no day
   book code; its rise is the day book's new strings in the reports catalogue
   chunk the two routes share (checked in the build: that chunk holds 99 day
   book keys). Both raised by measured + 0.3 KB in `bundle-budgets.json`. The
   app shell is unchanged (241.9 KB).

## Performance

`data/2026-10-24-day-book/dbab.sh` and `dbperf.mjs`: the production build of
dcaddbc (old) against this build (new), alternated over four rounds, six
opens per viewport per round, 4x CPU throttle, `/reports/day-book?period=thisMonth`
(a page of 100 lines on the QA book), timed from navigation to the first line
visible (a phone card or a table row, in both versions). n = 24 per cell.

| | old median (range) | new median (range) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone 390 | 1,525 ms (1,287–2,094) | 1,389 ms (1,250–1,834) | −8.9%, ranges overlap |
| Desktop 1280 | 2,417 ms (1,903–3,045) | 2,319 ms (1,704–3,187) | level (−4.1%) |

The phone median is lower and each card is two lines shorter, but the ranges
overlap almost entirely; recorded as level to slightly faster, not as a proven
gain. Desktop is level.
