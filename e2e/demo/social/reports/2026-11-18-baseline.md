## Findings
Each finding is laid out as problem / evidence / likely cause / fix / KPI / priority / how we measure.

**F1. We can't measure sign-ups or set-up.** Evidence: the only events are autocapture, pageview, survey and identify. There is no "code verified", "business created" or "first bill" event. Cause: `analyticsRules.ts` allows only a narrow set of events (DEC-017). Fix: add 3 named events: `otp_verified`, `business_created` and `first_bill_issued`. They carry no personal data; this needs an owner-approved code change. KPI: visitor → verified rate, and verified → first bill within 24 h. **Priority: Critical.** Measure: a PostHog funnel insight on these events.

**F2. UTMs are missing.** Evidence: 1 of 24 visitors had a utm_source, while LinkedIn sent 11. Cause: links shared without UTMs, or LinkedIn app redirects stripping them. Fix: put UTM links in every post body, check after the 19 Nov post goes out, and register UTMs as first-touch person properties. KPI: share of social sessions with utm_campaign (target >80%). **Priority: Critical.** Measure: HogQL by utm_campaign after 19 Nov.

**F3. The feedback survey nags visitors.** Evidence: "YourKhata Feedback" was shown 490 times, dismissed 67 times and answered 2 times (all-time). In 17–18 Nov it was shown 37 times to 21 people, nearly every visitor. Cause: no wait period or page targeting. Fix: show it only to signed-in users after 3+ sessions, or after onboarding, once per person; keep the teal tab for visitors. KPI: survey response rate (now 0.4%) and bounce on /. **Priority: High.** Measure: survey events.

**F4. Guide pages pull more than the home page.** Evidence: 16 people on /solutions* against 9 on /. Fix: link posts to the guide page for each job, and make sure each guide page has a clear "Start free" button to /login. KPI: guide → /login click rate. **Priority: High.** Measure: pageview paths.

**F5. Rage clicks on /signup.** Evidence: rage clicks on 14 and 17 Nov. /signup forwards to /login, so the redirect page may look dead for a moment. Fix: make old /signup links go straight to /login on the server; check session replays first. KPI: $rageclick on /signup = 0. **Priority: Medium.**

**F6. No search data yet.** Search Console is not available (see Sources). Fix: the owner opens GSC as kushwahaakash971, or adds this account as a restricted user. **Priority: High** (it blocks every SEO decision).
