Scope: `/reports/aging` with `book="shop"`: `AgingPageContent` (its Shop
branch), `AgingColumns` (a ⋯ column on the Shop's table), and the new
`AgingBarsCard`, `AgingRowSheet`, `AgingRowAction` and `view-model/agingCards.ts`.
The Udhaar book's `/ledger/aging` (`book="main"`) is deliberately unchanged:
it shares the component, and its revamp is the Udhaar piece the owner has not
started (24 Oct: "don't jump on Udhaar book"). The server is unchanged.

Who needs it: the owner before a collection round or before paying suppliers.
What it saves them: who has owed longest and how much, in the Shop's own book,
and the next act (remind, take the money, pay) from the same row without
finding the person again.

## What changed

- **The side in the Shop's words**: "Customers owe you" and "You owe
  suppliers", each with its own total; on a phone the total sits under the
  word and the two share the row.
- **Age bars** replace the four tiles: one hue, darker as money ages, with a
  Table view. Loaded with `dynamic()`, so the Udhaar book does not carry them.
- **Cards on a phone**: the contact in full, the oldest bucket in words (red
  past 90 days), and the total with "Owes you" or "You owe". A tap opens the
  row sheet.
- **The row sheet**: Remind and Receive payment for money to collect, Pay for
  money to pay, Open statement, then every bucket and the total. On a desktop
  table a ⋯ at the end of the row opens the same sheet.

## Found by looking and by the live run

1. **The second tab ran off the phone** ("You ow…" past the edge at 320–390):
   the Shop's side words are longer than the Udhaar book's. Fixed by stacking
   the total under the word and sharing the row.
2. Live, a customer who owes ₹2,074 in the Shop with no unpaid bill gets "This
   clears everything due" and "kept as advance for the next bill" in the same
   payment form. That is the existing payment form reading the dues from bills
   only; it is recorded here, not changed by this piece.
3. The QA book has every Shop due in 0–30 days, so the red 90+ chip is proved
   by the page test, not by the live run.

## Deviations and decisions recorded

1. **No tap-to-filter on the bars**: the aging read has no bucket parameter.
   A filter would need a backend change, which waits for approval.
2. **"90+ days", not "oldest 74 days"**: the aging read sends buckets, not
   the age of the oldest entry. A day count made from a bucket edge would be a
   number nobody stated.
3. **The sheet lists actions first**: `UbActionSheet` draws its action rows
   above its body; acting is the reason the sheet is opened.
4. **The reminder reads the contact's mobile first**: the aging rows carry no
   mobile, so Remind asks the contact's Shop page before opening the sheet.
5. **Bundle**: `/reports/aging` and `/ledger/aging` route chunk 101 → 102.9 KB
   (first load 343 → 344.7) for the cards, the pager and the sheet's loader;
   the bars and both flows are `dynamic()`. Raised by measured + 0.3 with a note.

## Performance

`data/2026-10-24-old-dues/odab.sh` and `odperf.mjs`: dcaddbc (old) against this
build, four alternating rounds, six opens per viewport per round, 4x CPU
throttle, `/reports/aging` (customers) on the QA book, timed from navigation to
the first row (card or table row) visible. n = 24 per cell.

| | old median (range) | new median (range) | |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone 390 | 1,108 ms (994–1,513) | 1,177 ms (988–1,501) | slower (+6.2%) |
| Desktop 1280 | 1,718 ms (1,169–4,539) | 1,299 ms (1,125–1,596) | faster (−24.4%) |

The phone is slower in every round (by 16 to 112 ms per round median), so it is
recorded as a real cost, not noise. Its cause is not traced: the route chunk
grew 1.9 KB and the age bars' chunk now loads beside the report's request. The
desktop gain matches the registers' and Profit's and is not claimed as this
piece's work.
