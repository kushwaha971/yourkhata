## What this means for our messaging (no claims about them)
- The category already teaches "ledger + WhatsApp reminder" (Khatabook) and
  "GST bill + stock" (Vyapar, myBillBook). Our honest angle is **both in one
  place, in the browser, with nothing to install, free right now**.
- Most rivals sell plans and trials. We may say "free right now, no card" and
  must not promise future pricing.
- Not yet researched (next cycle): their Instagram/YouTube formats, posting
  frequency and top-performing topics; user complaints are already in
  `docs/testing/edge-case-catalogue.md`.

## Gaps we can honestly use (we never name a competitor in posts)
- Rivals sell annual plans and trials. We are **free right now with no card**. That is a fact we can state today.
- Rivals are mostly apps to install. We run **in the browser with nothing to install**, and sign-in is a mobile number plus a WhatsApp code.
- Rivals compete on "X alternative" search pages. That SEO battlefield is crowded, so we should not enter it with comparison pages naming them; we use job-based pages instead.
