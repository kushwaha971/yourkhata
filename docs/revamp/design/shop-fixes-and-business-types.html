Bug fixes, your feedback, and what changes for each kind of shop

This page keeps the Shop exactly as it looks today. It only draws the parts that change: the problems in your screenshots, the four points parked in the backlog, and the new parts from the business-type plan (picking what you sell, scanning, sizes, batch and expiry, IMEI and the rest). Each fix shows your screenshot next to the fixed phone screen and the desktop screen.

Use Business type at the top to switch between eight kinds of shop. The menus, the words, the bill lines, the item form and the items list redraw for that shop. Dashed orange outlines mark a word or menu row that changed for that business. Everything else stays generic.

Part A

Fixes

Same colours, same fonts, same components. Only the layout faults are fixed. Each one names the component to change so the build later is one fix in one place, not screen by screen.

Your feedback

A1 · Header search

Today on phone the search is a small box with a down arrow. It opens a narrow list that covers half the screen, cuts the hint text off, and wraps every result onto two or three lines.

Now your screenshot
Fixed phone · search opens full screen
Fixed desktop 1280 · grouped list under the field

What changes

  1. Phone icon: a magnifier, not a box with a down arrow. A down arrow says "menu", not "search".
  2. Phone: tapping it opens a full-screen search with Back, the field at the top and the keyboard up. Results use the full width.
  3. Shorter hint: "Search name, item or bill no." fits at 320 px. The longer tip ("Try Radhe 2000…") moves under the field as one quiet line.
  4. Results in groups (Start, People, Items, Bills). Each row is one line of title plus one line of detail, cut with "…" instead of wrapping. The amount or phone sits on the right.
  5. Desktop: the list opens under the field at the same width (640 px), with keyboard hints at the bottom.
  6. "Nothing is saved yet" moves off every row into the Start group heading.

A2 · Folding sections (More details, Details)

Three faults in one component. When open, there is a second box inside the first, so the padding doubles. The grey header does not reach the border. The header has round corners at the bottom too, so a sliver of white shows in both bottom corners.

Now customer page · Details open
Fixed phone · one box, rows divided
Fixed desktop · same component
The three states, side by side

One rule for every folding section

  1. No inner box. What is inside a folding section is drawn bare. Groups inside are split by a 1 px line, with the same 16 px side padding as the header. On the customer page, Terms, Notes and Record become three rows of the one box.
  2. The header has no grey fill when open or closed. Grey is only the hover and press colour.
  3. Corners: the outer box clips its children (overflow: clip). The header button no longer has its own round corners, so a hover or focus colour fills right to the edge and into the corners.
  4. Focus ring goes on the whole box, not on the header, so it follows the outer corners (the Edit contact screenshot shows the ring cut by the body).
  5. Closed shows the summary under the title ("25/10/2026 · Maharashtra (27)"). Open keeps it, so you can still read it while editing.

A4 · Adding items to a bill

What changes

  1. One way in: the search bar with a scan button next to it. No empty line card is created. "+ Add item" goes, the search is always there.
  2. Each item is a short line: name and detail on the left, amount on the right; under it a − 1 + quantity control, the rate, and Edit. Edit opens the line sheet (unit, rate, discount, and the business's extra fields, see D2).

  4. Desktop: a table. Row one is the search ("Search or scan item · F2"). Qty, rate and discount are typed in the row.
  5. Empty bill: pay buttons stay off, and the line above them says why: "Add an item to finish the bill". If a field is wrong, the message names it and scrolls to it.

Part B

Business types

The business type picked at sign-up turns on only what that shop needs. A kirana sees nothing new. Words change only where a shop uses a different word (Distributor, Retailer). Menus that exist today stay; new rows appear only for the business that needs them. Switch the type at the top to see each one.

Part C

Scanning

For every business. On desktop the barcode scanner keeps working as it does today and gets a beep, a flash on the line, and a "not found" note. On phone the scan button opens the camera. On a bill it keeps scanning until Done; in a search or a barcode field it fills the field and closes.

1Change only words that differ, or also re-order menus per business?This page changes words (Distributor, Retailer, Company) and adds rows, but keeps today's order. Re-ordering per business (for example Bills first for a café) is possible later. Recommended: words and new rows only.
