`ProfitColumns`, the new `view-model/profitCards.ts` and `ProfitRowSheet`) and

bills earn it, readable on a phone without a cut name, and each row's whole

## What changed

- **The answer first**: Sales (with its bill count), Cost ("what it cost you"),
  Profit or Loss, and Margin, in the shared ruled figure box, with the basis
  line under it. The expenses and the profit after expenses sit in their own
  small panel beneath, because they answer a second question.
- **Cards on a phone**: the name in full, what it covers (quantity and bills
  for an item, date and customer for a bill), "Sold ₹x", and Profit or Loss
  with the margin under it. A tap opens the row sheet; its Open reaches the
  item, the customer's Shop page or the bill.
- **Search on a phone** sits above the cards and stays one input, so typing
  never loses focus while the server answers.

## Found by looking and by the live run

1. **A 59-character cable name was cut** by the two-line clamp on both the card
   and the table cell; names now wrap in full (`break-words`), which also
   changes the shared card row for the day book, registers and GST.
2. The harness's search check first read the wrong box and read before the
   answer arrived; fixed in the harness, not the page.

## Deviations and decisions recorded

1. **The group stays a dropdown**, not chips: the owner's 6 Oct filter rule
   puts a choice of more than three in a select.
2. **No "By day"**: the server has no day grouping. Adding one is a backend
   change awaiting approval.
3. **Category rows open nothing**: there is no category page to open.
4. **No Print**, as for every report; it belongs in `ReportPageShell` once.
5. **The link to fix costs is not built**: the banner keeps its sentence. Where
   to send the owner (item edit, or a list of uncosted items) is a separate
   decision.
6. **Bundle**: `/reports/profit` route raised to 113.3 KB (first load 355.2);
   registers 117.9/359.7, day book 119.5/361.3, GST 118.5/360.4 and `/imports`
   first load 299.2 moved through the regrouped shared report chunk. Raised by
   measured + 0.3 with a note.
