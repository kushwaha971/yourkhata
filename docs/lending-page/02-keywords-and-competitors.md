# Lending page — keywords and competitors (research 17 Nov 2026)

These are public search results only. No volume figures are given, because we
have no paid SEO tool. Rankings and clicks are measured after launch in Search
Console.

## Search intent and keywords

| Intent | Keywords / questions | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| Commercial (primary) | daily collection app, loan collection app, daily finance app, money lending app for small business, loan register app | Page title, H1, hero |
| Commercial (secondary) | weekly collection loan app, monthly loan tracker, route-wise collection, collection book app Hindi | H2s and feature blocks |
| Informational / long-tail | how to track loans and repayments, how to manage partial repayments, how to record a missed instalment, how to make a daily loan schedule that skips Sundays, how to send a payment reminder on WhatsApp | Guides and FAQ |

## Competitors (self-described; not hands-on tested)

| Product | What it says it does | Our honest position |
|---|---|---|
| [VasoolX](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vasoolx.app) | Loan app for individual moneylenders; daily, weekly and monthly loans | **Same core.** Unproven which is easier until we test it hands-on. |
| [OnePlaceHQ Cash Loan](https://oneplace.pages.dev/cashloan) | Upfront-cut and daily-interest loans, schedule skips Sundays and holidays | They skip holidays; **we skip only Sundays.** Upfront cut ≈ our "charges". |
| [Jainam Software](https://appgoblin.info/apps/annualproject.com.jainamsoftware) | Agent collection, SMS receipts, full accounting | They send SMS; **we send nothing automatically** (by design). |
| [Daily Collection – Week, Month](https://appgoblin.info/apps/com.sht.fcn) | Daily, weekly and monthly loans; agents collect | Ad-supported. **We show no ads.** |
| Khatabook / [OkCredit](https://okcredit.in/udhar-khata-app) | Udhaar ledgers; both also offer or connect to loans for merchants | They are credit ledgers, not collection schedules. **We give no credit.** |

**What we can honestly say is different:**
1. The full schedule, what you collect and what you earn are shown before saving.
2. Late, due-today and not-paid status come from the schedule itself.
3. A wrong entry is removed with a reason and stays visible.
4. One login also runs a Shop, a Money book or a Library, each with its own separate book.
5. English and Hindi.

**Gaps to state plainly:**
- no holiday calendar;
- no interest-rate field;
- no automatic SMS;
- no restriction of a collector to their own route.
