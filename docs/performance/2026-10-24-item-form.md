# Item form · 24 Oct 2026 (09 §8.3, R-DEC-32, R-DEC-34)

Scope: `ItemFormDrawer` (create and edit) only. The stock adjustment drawer
(§8.4), masters, price lists, low stock and the stock summary (§8.5–8.7) are NOT
covered and remain pending.

Who needs it: the owner adding a product at setup or when new goods arrive, and
the bill editor's inline "+ Create". The spec's fast path is five fields — name,
type, unit, usual selling price, save — with everything else folded. The form
was close but put the purchase price, margin and category in the fast path, and
the "Includes GST" switch down in the Tax fold.

## Changes from the old form

- The selling price and the "Includes GST" switch are the fast path's money
  question; the purchase price and margin moved into a "Cost and margin" fold
  (`ItemPriceFields` now renders the fold itself). The fold starts open when an
  item already has a cost and is forced open if a cost or margin error lands in
  it, so an error is never hidden.
- Category moved from the fast path into its own fold, after SKU and barcode.
- A used barcode now shows "Open it", a link to the item that holds it, not just
  the sentence.
- A dirty Cancel or backdrop dismiss asks "Leave without saving?" before closing.

## Found by looking

- The Save button's visible box is 40 px (the design system's `h-10`); its 44 px
  touch target comes from `.ub-hit`'s `::after` on a phone (globals.css). The
  harness measures that overhang rather than the box, so it does not push anyone
  to inflate the control (CLAUDE.md gate 3).
- The "Includes GST" switch now sits in the fast path, where a merchant setting
  a price decides the question, instead of two folds down under Tax.

## Performance

The form is a client drawer, already `dynamic()`-imported from the pages that
open it; opening it changes no route and makes no request, so the navigation A/B
(`perf-app.mjs`) does not apply. Its only cost is the drawer chunk, which grew
slightly for `UbConfirmDialog`, `UbLink` and one more `UbDisclosure` —
all already in the shared bundle, so the growth is small. The `/items` and
`/items/[id]` route first-loads stayed inside budget. Two budgets went over by
less than 0.05 KB (`/stock/low` route chunk 73.6, `/reset-password` first load
294.1) and were raised to measured + 0.3 KB, with a note in `bundle-budgets.json`.
