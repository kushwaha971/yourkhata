## Medium — rewrite of "YourKhata: One Honest Book for Your Whole Business"

The October post says several things that are no longer true: a four-digit
code (it is six digits on WhatsApp), "Hindi and English throughout", "one
customer, one balance everywhere" (each app now keeps its own money), an
offline desktop edition "being built", "see the profit on that bill", and
khata screens that were removed. Proposed replacement, same URL:

**Title:** YourKhata: Your Shop's Records, on Your Phone
**Subtitle:** GST bills, stock and who owes you — in one place, for every kind of business in India.
**Lead image:** `medium-cover-1600x840.png`
**Tags:** GST, Small Business → *Business*, Inventory Management, Retail, India

> ### The ₹500 nobody can prove
>
> It's the last week of the month. A customer is sure they paid ₹500 last
> Tuesday. The owner is sure they didn't. The proof, if it exists, is spread
> across a notebook, a bill book, a stock register and a WhatsApp chat.
>
> YourKhata (yourkhata.com) puts those in one place.
>
> ### Six jobs, done in a few taps
>
> **Make a GST bill.** Search an item, set the quantity, issue. GST is worked
> out on every line — CGST and SGST for a customer in your state, IGST for one
> in another.
>
> **Keep credit straight.** Bill on credit and see what each customer owes,
> bill by bill.
>
> **Remind who owes you.** Tap Remind and a WhatsApp message is ready with the
> exact amount. You check it and press send; nothing goes without you.
>
> **Know your stock.** It goes down with every bill and up with every
> purchase. Low items show on Home.
>
> **Record purchases.** Supplier bills with input GST, paid now or later.
>
> **Close the day.** Day book, cash book, money to collect and the GST summary
> for your accountant.
>
> ### Built for the business you actually run
>
> Every kind of business gets its own words, features and a guide page with
> real screens: yourkhata.com/solutions.
>
> ### Things you can count on
>
> - **Nothing to install.** It opens in the browser on any phone or computer.
> - **No password.** Your mobile number and a 6-digit code on WhatsApp.
> - **Your name on your bills.** Bills, receipts and statements carry your
>   business's name and logo, never ours.
> - **Your staff, your rules.** Each person gets their own login and role.
> - **Your data stays yours.** Export lists to CSV, or download everything
>   from Settings, any time.
>
> ### Start free
>
> Enter your mobile number at **yourkhata.com**, type the code from WhatsApp,
> pick your kind of shop, and make your first bill. No card needed.
