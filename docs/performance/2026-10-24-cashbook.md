# Cash book · 24 Oct 2026 (08 §11, R-DEC-32, R-DEC-34)

Scope: `/cashbook` (`CashbookPageContent`, `CashbookFigures`,
`CashbookClosePanel`, `CashbookDayCard`), and the two openers it gained: the
expense detail sheet by id (`useExpenseForm.openDetailById`,
`expenseReadThunk`) and the prefilled expense drawer
(`useExpenseForm.openPrefilled`). The server is unchanged.

Who needs it: the owner at closing time asking "how much cash should be in
the drawer, and how much in the bank?", and the accountant once a month.
What it saves them: the four figures without a tap, the drawer count against
the book in one field, and the fix for a difference in the same place,
already filled in.

## What changed

- **The four figures are always in view.** They were folded into a
  "Details" summary line like a list's totals; on this screen they are the
  answer, so they are a ruled 2 × 2 box on a phone and one line of four on a
  desktop. In is green; a closing below zero says what it means.
- **Cash · Bank · Both** chips instead of a "Cash or bank" dropdown, beside
  the period.
- **Close the day** asks "Count the cash in your drawer", says Matches,
  Short by or Extra as the merchant types, and offers the fix that difference
  points at, filled in: the missing expense with the shortfall in cash, or the
  forgotten payment with the extra in cash. It says the count is not saved.
  After the payment is saved the cash book reads itself again, so the same
  count turns to "Matches".
- **Rows open.** A payment row opens its receipt; an expense row opens its
  detail sheet (and its Cancel expense). A khata line has nothing to open
  here and is not a button.
- **Desktop:** the days on the left; close the day and where the money went
  in a rail on the right.
- **Words.** Payments out read "Paid to …" (they read "Given to", the Udhaar
  book's word); "voided" is "cancelled"; "Bank & UPI" is "Bank"; "Both";
  the first-use and quiet-range sentences are the spec's.

## Found by looking and by the live run

1. **Phone rows squeezed the title to a third of the row** and split
   "PAYOUT/26-27/2034" over two lines, because the running "Cash after"
   figure sat beside the amount. It is now a caption line under the row, and
   each caption piece stays whole.
2. **"Where the money went" read 1% for every category**: its share was of
   all money out, which includes supplier payments. It is the share of
   expenses now (28%, 26%, …).
3. **At 320 px the minus left its number**: "Expected cash in drawer: -" on
   one line and "₹15,72,421.24" on the next, and the same in "Cash after".
   Figures in sentences use a non-breaking minus, and the harness checks
   signed amounts as it checks receipt numbers.
4. **The Hindi day header said "Sat"**: the weekday was formatted with
   `hi-IN` only when the locale was exactly `hi`, and the screen's locale is
   `hi-IN`. It uses the shared `formattingLocale` now.
5. **The bucket chips lost their 44 px targets and "Both" ran off a 320 px
   phone**: inside the period picker's row, which on a phone is one
   sideways-scrolling line that clips. They sit beside it and wrap under it.
6. **The cash book did not move after "Receive a payment you forgot"**:
   recording a payment never told the cash book. The screen that opens the
   drawer now re-reads itself on save (a page test fails without it); the
   payment's invalidation is left as it was, since a payment recorded
   anywhere else is read fresh when the cash book is next opened.

## Deviations and decisions recorded

1. **The period is the shared dropdown, not a row of chips** (owner, 6 Oct
   2026: the period is one button), as on Expenses and Payments. Last month
   stays in it: it was there, and removing it was not asked for.
2. **The two fixes are split by the difference.** The spec lists "Add the
   missing expense" and "Receive a payment you forgot" under "Short by".
   Receiving a payment RAISES the expected cash, so offered for a shortfall it
   would widen the gap; it is offered for an extra amount instead.
3. **Dates are dd/mm/yyyy** ("as on 01/10/2026"), the product's date rule,
   not "as on 1 Oct".
4. **No partial-failure state.** The server computes every source in one
   request and answers all or an error; there is no per-source failure for
   the screen to name. Building one is backend work and is not done here.
5. **The page still scrolls** rather than fitting the viewport like a
   register: it is a day book of collapsible days with a panel, not a paged
   table, and 08 §11 asks for neither a pager nor an inner scroll.
6. **Bundle:** the app shell's baseline rounds 241.8 → 241.9 KB with
   framework and shared app unchanged (127.3 / 114.6) and no cash book code in
   any shared chunk (checked by name). The new read thunk and the money
   helper were put in modules of their own, away from files the store and
   the payments view-model import; rebuilding showed that did not change the
   figure, and removing the invalidation change did not either. Seven first-load limits that were over by less than
   0.1 KB use measured + 0.3 KB, recorded in `bundle-budgets.json`. The
   likely cause, inferred and not traced, is the runtime chunk map gaining the
   new lazy chunks.
7. **The QA book's cash is negative** (−₹15.7 lakh in the drawer) because
   the seeded data has no opening anchor; the screen shows it honestly and
   names it.
